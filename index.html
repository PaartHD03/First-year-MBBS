Recommended textbooks

Check the edition and preferred books with the department or library before buying.

🦴 Anatomy

Core options: B.D. Chaurasia; Vishram Singh.
Atlas: Netter's Atlas; Gray's Anatomy for Students.
Histology/Embryology: use the faculty-recommended text.

❤️ Physiology

Core options: Sembulingam; Guyton & Hall.
Reference: Ganong's Review of Medical Physiology.

🧬 Biochemistry

Core options: D.M. Vasudevan; Satyanarayana.
Reference: Harper's Illustrated Biochemistry.
