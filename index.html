The SUHANI Method

A little framework for smarter learning.

Learn with purpose. Grow with consistency.

Medical school is a journey of small efforts repeated every day. Follow these six simple principles to make your learning more meaningful.

S

Study with focus

Give your full attention to what you are learning. Quality matters more than hours.

U

Understand concepts

Build a strong foundation instead of simply memorising information.

H

Habituate consistency

Make learning a daily habit. Small efforts add up over time.

A

Apply what you learn

Connect theory with diagrams, practicals, clinical examples and questions.

N

Never stop improving

Learn from mistakes, revise difficult topics and keep challenging yourself.

I

Improve every day

Be a little better than yesterday. Progress matters more than perfection.

Recommended textbooks

Check the edition and preferred books with the department or library before buying.

🦴 Anatomy

Core options: B.D. Chaurasia; Vishram Singh.
Atlas: Netter's Atlas; Gray's Anatomy for Students.
Histology/Embryology: Use the faculty-recommended text.

❤️ Physiology

Core options: Sembulingam; Guyton & Hall.
Reference: Ganong's Review of Medical Physiology.

🧬 Biochemistry

Core options: D.M. Vasudevan; Satyanarayana.
Reference: Harper's Illustrated Biochemistry.
