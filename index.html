A LITTLE SOMETHING SPECIAL

The SUHANI Method ❤️

A little method, with a very special name.

S

Study with focus

Give your full attention to what you are learning.

U

Understand concepts

Build a strong foundation instead of just memorising.

H

Habituate consistency

Make learning a daily habit. Small efforts add up.

A

Apply what you learn

Connect theory with diagrams, practicals and questions.

N

Never stop improving

Learn from mistakes and revise difficult topics.

I

Improve every day

Be a little better than yesterday. Progress over perfection.
